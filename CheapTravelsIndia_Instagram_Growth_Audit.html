Section 7 · Conversion

The Call / WhatsApp Booking Funnel

You asked the most important question: how do we get more calls and messages to actually book? Reach is worthless if it doesn't turn into a WhatsApp "Hi". Here's the funnel that converts a scroll into a booking.

Reel reach (new people)→ Profile visit→ WhatsApp "Hi"→ Fare quote→ Booking + e-ticket

The 7 levers that turn views into WhatsApp messages

Lever 1

Put the CTA on the video, not just the caption

Last frame of every reel: "WhatsApp 8853521184 — ticket 90 sec" with a WhatsApp icon. Most viewers never read captions.

Lever 2

Pre-filled WhatsApp link everywhere

Bio link + Story sticker open a chat with "Route: ___ Date: ___" pre-typed. Removing typing friction is the single biggest conversion win.

Lever 3

Reply in under 5 minutes

Speed = trust for a first-timer sending money. Use WhatsApp quick-reply templates for fares so you answer instantly.

Lever 4

Comment-to-DM automation

"Comment FARE and we'll DM the price." Comments boost reach and capture leads. (Manychat/free tools do this.)

Lever 5

Story funnel daily

Poll ("Delhi ya Varanasi?"), then a "DM to book" sticker. Stories reach your warmest audience — your existing followers.

Lever 6

Proof kills hesitation

Post delivered e-tickets, PNRs, and "refund in 30 min" screenshots. A nervous buyer needs to see others trusted you.

Lever 7

Save the number after every booking

Broadcast list (with consent) for festival fares & return-journey reminders. Repeat bookings are your cheapest growth.
