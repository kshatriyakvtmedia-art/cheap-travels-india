Travel Entrepreneur • Varanasi, UP

Born and raised on the banks of the Ganga in Varanasi, Samar Pratap Singh has spent years transforming how people experience the sacred cities of Uttar Pradesh. With an intimate knowledge of every ghat, temple, hidden lane and heritage site, he founded Cheap Travels India with one powerful vision.

That vision: luxury travel should not be a privilege of the wealthy. Every traveler — whether a pilgrim arriving from a small village or a corporate group from a metro city — deserves clean rooms, comfortable cabs, expert guidance, and memories that last a lifetime.

Today, Cheap Travels India manages large multi-city group tours, corporate retreats, school trips, and intimate family pilgrimages — with the same personal attention every time.
